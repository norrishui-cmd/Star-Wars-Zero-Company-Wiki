Build a four-slot squad from story characters and custom recruits. Use the role summary to see who can heal, fight at range or move an enemy out of cover. Some missions let you control additional characters.

Roles on this board

Hawks must join story missions. Skirmishes allow any available combination. The board shows the selected recruits’ specialties and the story characters’ identifying roles. Your equipped gear, current Specializations and Bonds can change what each Operator brings.
