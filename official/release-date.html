Star Wars Zero Company Release Date and Platforms

Star Wars Zero Company released on August 27, 2026. The single-player tactics game is available on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Available now

Where to get the game

  • PC: EA app, Steam, and Epic Games Store.
  • PlayStation: PlayStation 5.
  • Xbox: Xbox Series X|S.

EA lists physical Standard Edition copies for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at selected retailers. Check your region and platform before buying.

Steam and PC requirements

The Steam version runs without installing the EA app, according to EA. Steam separately lists account-linking requirements. The PC listing specifies 50 GB of storage and 16 GB RAM minimum, or 32 GB recommended. Use the official release guide for the complete specifications.

Sources: EA release guide, EA game FAQ, Steam store listing.
