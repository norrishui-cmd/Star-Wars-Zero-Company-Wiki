The Den · Updated November 17, 2026

Personnel at the Den

Open Personnel between missions to inspect your Operators. Start with the pair’s Bond when you want to train two teammates, or open an individual Operator’s progression options when you want to change their role.

Where to check a Bond

In Personnel, look up the relationship between the two Operators you want to deploy. Both can benefit from Cross Training when their Bond increases. At maximum Bond they can unlock a Passive through advanced Cross Training. See how to raise Bonds and check the level-4 modifier if a reward is missing.

What to check when an option is locked

Review the Operator’s Talent and tactical Specialization in their Focus Tree. A new campaign can have separate rules for changes to Talents, primary and secondary Specializations, and weapons. Which rule did you enable? Use the respec and lockout guide to match the disabled option to its setting. Weapon changes are handled in the Armory.

Adding someone to the roster

Recruitment is a separate facility. If you want another Operator, check your free roster slots, then open Recruitment to review candidates and the hiring cost. Return to Personnel once you have a pair to train.
