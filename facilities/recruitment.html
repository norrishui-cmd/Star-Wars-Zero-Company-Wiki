How to Recruit Operators in Star Wars Zero Company

Open Recruitment at the Den to hire a Custom Operator. Patch 1.2 sets the price at 100 Credits per recruit, regardless of roster size. You need a free roster slot. EA's Patch 1.2 notes

How to hire a Custom Operator

  1. At the Den, open Recruitment and check your available roster capacity and Credits.
  2. Pick a candidate whose Talent and Specialization fit a job you need covered. You can also create a Custom Operator in the Character Databank from the main menu and select that Operator from Recruitment while their Databank entry is active.
  3. Hire the Operator for 100 Credits. Review their Talent and Specialization in Personnel, weapon in the Armory, and appearance in Customization. Campaign modifiers can restrict Talent, Specialization and weapon changes.

EA's recruitment and customization guide · EA's respec modifier notes

How to refresh candidates

Under the default rules, use Refresh Recruits to see a new selection. With the optional Locked Recruitment Refresh modifier enabled, the button is unavailable. The pool still refreshes automatically at the start of each Cycle. EA's recruitment refresh rules

When to recruit or expand your roster

Hire for a role you would otherwise leave empty or for a backup when a key Operator is recovering. Check the next mission and current recovery queue before you spend. If your company is at capacity, check whether the Upgrade Facility offers an Increased Crew Quarters project. In Patch 1.2 these upgrades cost 0 Credits, and construction still uses a Build Slot.
