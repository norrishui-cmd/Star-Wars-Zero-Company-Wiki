Star Wars Zero Company Campaign Modifiers Guide

To enable Campaign Modifiers, start a new campaign and open Additional Campaign Options in Difficulty Settings. Patch 1.2 lets you use modifiers at any difficulty. The selected rules take effect after the tutorial.

How to enable Campaign Modifiers

  1. From the main menu, choose New Campaign.
  2. On Difficulty Settings, choose your difficulty and review the Permadeath and Beskar Mode settings for this save.
  3. Open Additional Campaign Options and select the rules you want.
  4. Start the campaign and finish the tutorial to activate the modifiers.

Campaign Modifiers apply to the new save. If you already have a campaign underway, create a separate campaign to play with modifiers. For the regular setup choices, see the difficulty and Beskar Mode guide.

Which settings change the game?

What to check before confirming your campaign
RuleEffect to consider
Injury limitWith Two Strikes, an Operator who already has an Injury can die when defeated in a Tactical Mission. The default three-strike threshold is two existing Injuries. Instant Death can make the first tactical defeat fatal. EA documents a Trick exception in the patch notes.
Random death riskNo Risk of Death leaves the injury limit in effect and adds no random death chance. Static risk adds a 20% chance on tactical defeat. Escalating risk begins at 20%, rises to 40% after one survived defeat, and reaches 80% after two.
Health growthBy default, Operators gain 1 maximum HP for every 3 Focus Points earned. Reduced growth requires 6 Focus Points for that 1 HP. The no-growth setting removes this source of maximum HP.
Advantage generationYou can exclude area attacks or assist attacks from earning Advantage, or make eligible damaging attacks generate it only half the time. Fewer gains mean fewer opportunities to spend Advantage on special actions.
Tactical rulesAssist Shots Can Miss makes assists use normal ranged hit rules. Other settings add enemy critical hits or cap Operator ranged hit chance at 99%.
Roster and respecSeparate settings can lock changes to Talents, Specializations, or weapons. The recruitment lock removes manual refresh, with the scheduled refresh still happening each cycle.

The added random death risk applies to defeat in Tactical Missions. An injury picked up in a Galaxy Map Operation does not trigger that death roll or raise the escalating chance. Use the Permadeath and injury guide when choosing a threshold.

Three settings combinations to try

These are suggested combinations. EA recommends learning the default rules on Normal before enabling modifiers.

Practice a backup plan

Enable Assist Shots Can Miss and turn off Advantage generation from assist attacks. Keep the standard injury and health settings. Before relying on an assist, check the hit preview and reserve a teammate's Action Points for a follow-up if the shot misses.

Make injuries matter sooner

With Permadeath on, choose Two Strikes and No Risk of Death. An already injured Operator faces a lower margin for error in tactical combat, with the injury threshold controlling the risk. Check the Medbay and your available substitutes between missions.

Commit to your squad's roles

Enable Locked Talent Respec and Locked Tactical Respec. Leave weapon changes and manual recruit refresh available. Pick complementary roles before you deploy and use weapon or lineup changes to cover gaps. See the first squad guide for role ideas.
