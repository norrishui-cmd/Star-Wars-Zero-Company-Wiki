How to Build Bonds and Unlock Cross Training

At the Den, open Personnel and check the Bond between the two Operators you want to train. A Bond belongs to a pair. Gaining a Bond level can give both Operators a permanent combat bonus through Cross Training.

How to raise a pair’s Bond

  1. Check the pair in Personnel before choosing the next deployment. Note their current level so you can tell when it changes.
  2. Give them opportunities to work together. Operations, Tactical Missions and Call for Backup can all contribute. Choose a mission where both Operators’ combat roles are useful.
  3. Check Personnel again between missions. Story progress also affects Bonds. During a Dilemma, Hawks’ choice can improve a relationship with one Operator while weakening another.

Why a Bond increase may give no Cross Training

Patch 1.2 added a new-campaign option called No Cross Trainings Between Disliked Pairs. With it enabled, the game withholds Cross Training from Bond level increases below level 4. For example, reaching level 3 under this rule does not grant the pair a training reward. Check the pair again when it reaches level 4.

The level-4 restriction comes from this optional rule. For campaigns using the default settings, check the pair’s actual Bond increase and available reward in Personnel. See how to choose Campaign Modifiers for the setup screen and when those rules take effect.

What changes at maximum Bond?

A regular Cross Training grants a permanent combat stat benefit to both Operators. Reaching maximum Bond unlocks advanced Cross Training with a Passive ability. Bond progression can also earn Focus Points for improving Talents and Abilities.

When assigning your next mission, compare the benefit of training that pair with the jobs the objective requires. If you need a different squad to finish the mission, you can return to the pair on a later deployment.
