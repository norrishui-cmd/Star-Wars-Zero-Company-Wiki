Star Wars Zero Company Den Upgrades, Recruitment and Medbay

Before spending Credits at the Den, check who can deploy and what the next Tactical Mission requires. Patch 1.2 sets recruitment at 100 Credits per Custom Operator and makes the four Increased Crew Quarters upgrades cost 0 Credits each. EA's Patch 1.2 notes

What to upgrade first at the Den

  • Your roster is full and you need another role. Queue the next available Increased Crew Quarters upgrade. It costs 0 Credits in Patch 1.2, and construction still needs an open Build Slot and at least one Cycle. If there is room already, use Recruitment to fill the gap.
  • Injuries keep sidelining your regular squad. Assign treatment and pick a healthy substitute for the next mission. Consider an extra Medical Bed if treatment capacity repeatedly delays recovery.
  • Your roster is healthy and has the roles you need. Upgrade a weapon class your regular Operators already use. If you plan to switch classes, save the slot for a facility improvement that helps the current squad.

Some upgrades open new options. The Droid Bay enables recruitment of Custom Astromech Operators, for example. Finish a Tactical Mission to advance the Cycle. EA's overview of the Den · Patch 1.2 prices

Selected costs in Patch 1.2

  • Recruit a Custom Operator for 100 Credits.
  • Build Increased Crew Quarters I, II, III or IV for 0 Credits each.
  • Build Medical Bed III or IV for 500 Credits each.
  • Buy Rifle Range I or II for 1,500 Credits each.
  • Buy Combat Readiness I, II or III for 2,000 Credits each.

Medical Bed prices cover the upgrade. Check the Medbay for the cost to treat an individual Operator. EA's upgrade and recruitment changes

Recruit for a role you need

Open Recruitment at the Den when you have a free roster slot and 100 Credits. Pick a backup for an Operator you rely on, or add a role you need for upcoming missions. Keep the company roster separate from the squad you send into combat. EA describes a starting capacity of seven and a maximum company size of 20, with room added through upgrades and campaign progression. EA's squad guide · Bit Reactor's roster capacity Q&A

The candidate list updates at the start of a Cycle. If you enabled Locked Recruitment Refresh for your campaign, wait for that automatic update. Under the default rules, you can also use the manual Refresh Recruits button. Use Personnel to review Specializations, Bonds and available Cross Training, and the Armory to adjust equipment. EA's Campaign Modifiers notes · EA's Operator customization guide

Use the Medbay before the next mission

The Medbay treats Injuries from Operations and Tactical Missions. Standard beds take one Cycle, and a Bacta Tank can heal immediately when available. Treatment costs Credits. If a healthy substitute covers the role, use a bed and rotate them in. Consider the Bacta Tank when an injured Operator is needed on the next mission and your Medbay offers it. EA's Medbay overview

Before leaving the Den, check the HoloTable for expiring Operations, assign recovery, then start any construction you want progressing during the next Cycle. Review your deployment after changes to the roster. EA's Operations and Tactical Missions FAQ
