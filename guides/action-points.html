Star Wars Zero Company Action Points (AP) Guide

Each Operator starts with three Action Points (AP) each squad turn. Your Operators act before the enemy takes its turn. Check the movement and action previews before committing so you know what each Operator can still do from the destination.

What costs AP?

  • Movement. AP cost increases with distance. Preview the cost of each destination and check the cover and line of sight at the tile before you move.
  • Attacks and abilities. Weapon class and the selected action affect the cost. Check the action icon and the hit-chance preview before spending AP.
  • Utility items. Most cost 1 AP. Check the item tooltip before using a Medpac or other utility.
  • Call for Backup. The selected Operator can request an assist for their next attack once that turn. The request costs 0 AP. Budget for the attack itself.
  • Overwatch. Commit remaining AP to cover a cone during the enemy turn. Setting Overwatch immediately ends that Operator's turn. Your other Operators can still act before you choose End Turn.

How much AP do blaster attacks cost?

EA's weapon guide lists these costs for the standard attacks. Check the current action preview if an ability or effect changes what the Operator can do.

  • Blaster Rifle. The first shot costs 1 AP. Additional shots in the same turn cost 2 AP each.
  • Blaster Pistol. Each shot costs 1 AP.
  • Longarm Blaster. Each shot costs 2 AP.
  • Repeater. Spending more AP on its attack increases Rapid Fire damage.

Two example AP budgets

These examples use hypothetical tile costs and EA's published weapon rules. Check the previews for your Operator and map.

Blaster Rifle from a covered tile

At 3 AP, an Operator who already has a clear shot from cover can fire twice for 1 + 2 AP. If reaching cover costs 1 AP first, the first shot leaves only 1 AP. The second shot requires 2 AP, so consider a utility or Overwatch if the preview permits it.

Longarm Blaster after moving

A tile previewed at 1 AP leaves the 2 AP needed for a Longarm shot, provided the target is in range and visible from there. A tile previewed at 2 AP leaves just 1 AP. Pick a nearer tile that preserves a useful shot, or reposition this Operator and use another teammate for the current attack.

What can you do after spending AP?

Before ending the squad turn, check whether another Operator still has an action available. Advantage is a shared resource that powers special actions. Some of those actions can grant an Operator extra AP, and you can spend Advantage even when the Operators have used their AP. Check the Advantage guide for when to spend it.

The game can warn you about unused AP when you choose End Turn. In the main menu, go to Options → Gameplay → General and enable Warn For Early End Turn if you want the reminder.

Patch 1.2 note. The Astromech's Built-In Comlink can still grant AP to allies. EA lists a limit of three uses per turn at maximum rank.
