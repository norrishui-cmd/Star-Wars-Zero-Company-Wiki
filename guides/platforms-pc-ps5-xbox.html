Star Wars Zero Company PC Requirements, Steam, PS5 and Xbox

Star Wars Zero Company is available on Windows PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. On PC, EA lists 16 GB of RAM and 50 GB of free space for the minimum settings. The Steam release runs without the EA app. EA's official FAQ

If your hardware is close to the minimum, start with the Low preset at 1080p. For Intel Arc cards, EA recommends enabling Resizable BAR. EA's PC requirements guide

Does the Steam version need the EA app?

No. EA describes the Steam version as Steam-native. The Steam listing's terms separately list an internet connection and linked Steam and EA accounts. EA's general PC specs leave out an online-connection requirement for the single-player game. If offline play is important, check the storefront's setup and activation requirements before buying. EA's Steam FAQ · Steam listing and terms

EA says the game launched without Steam Deck Verified status. Check the compatibility label in Steam before buying for a Deck. EA's Steam Deck FAQ

Where to buy on PC, PS5 and Xbox

  • On Windows PC, choose Steam, the EA app, or Epic Games Store. Steam lists achievements and Steam Cloud saves. For another PC store, check its launcher and account terms.
  • On PlayStation 5, choose a digital copy from PlayStation Store or check selected retailers for a physical Standard Edition.
  • On Xbox Series X|S, choose the digital Xbox version. Selected retailers also carry a physical Standard Edition for consoles that can read discs.

Standard includes the base game. Deluxe adds cosmetic packs and weapon themes. If you want those cosmetics later, Steam lists a separate Deluxe Edition Upgrade. EA's platform and edition selector · Steam edition listings

Before you buy or install

  1. On Windows, run dxdiag. Compare the System tab's CPU and RAM and the Display tab's GPU with the table. Check free space on the drive you plan to install to.
  2. Pick the platform where you plan to keep playing. Verify the store's account and launcher requirements if you use several devices.
  3. If you plan to use a controller on PC, keep a keyboard available. EA lists support for PS5 and Xbox Series controllers, with keyboard entry needed for some text fields.
