Cast evidence file

Nicole Rainteau as Tel-Rea Vokoss

Official answer: Nicole Rainteau is the credited performer for Tel-Rea Vokoss.

Identity snapshot

Tel-Rea Vokoss is described as the Tognath Jedi Padawan and authored Operator Tel-Rea.

Tel-Rea is the only Jedi Padawan-class Operator; the official Q&A says another cannot be recruited if she dies.

Production snapshot

The credit appeared in the July 23 pre-SDCC cast feature. It verifies the pairing without assigning unannounced mechanics to the character.
