How to read the credits

A voice credit confirms that a character appears in the production. It does not automatically mean that the character is recruitable, playable or present in every campaign. Hawks has two credited voice options because Hawks is customizable; support staff such as Runa, Bennic, Neesh and Meevo are associated with The Den; other newly named roles still await fuller official descriptions.

Source and evidence boundary

The direct answer on this page is grounded in a first-party publication. It does not convert unreleased values, inferred story beats or marketing footage into confirmed gameplay data. Any development-stage value will be checked again against the launch build.
