## Quality controls

- Every new page has a direct answer and a first-party citation.
- Unknown weapon names, damage values, skill trees and mission rewards are not
  invented.
- Cast pages do not imply that every credited character is playable.
- Mission category pages do not pretend to be walkthroughs before maps exist.
- Strategy pages identify developer-confirmed interactions without presenting
  them as universal tier rankings.
- New English pages contain at least 202 visible words.
- New German pages contain at least 191 visible words after the final pass.
- New Japanese pages contain at least 289 CJK characters.
- Five-gram similarity of main content stays below 0.67 after shared site chrome
  and source boilerplate are excluded.

## Path to 1,000 quality URLs

The remaining gap is 506 indexable URLs. The expansion ceiling is not a quota;
each batch must pass the same evidence and concrete-answer gate.

1. Localize the 50 verified FAQ and 80 active news pages fully into German and
   Japanese, after removing any stale pre-Q&A wording.
2. Translate the strongest database, system, facility, species and cosmetic
   pages where a true English equivalent already passes the quality gate.
3. Convert official SDCC and later first-party reveals into new entity,
   mechanic and media-analysis clusters only when the source adds unique facts.
4. Keep speculative builds, enemy combinations and campaign routes outside the
   sitemap until the released game provides reproducible data.
